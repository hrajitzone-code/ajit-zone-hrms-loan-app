How to Connect Your Google Sheet (3 Easy Steps)

Follow these 3 quick steps to link any Google Sheet as your live database in less than 1 minute:

1
Create or Open a Google Sheet

Open Apps Script & Paste the Connector Code

3
Deploy as Web App & Paste URL Here
  • Click the blue Deploy $\rightarrow$ New Deployment button.
  • Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  • Set Execute as: Me
  • Set Who has access: Anyone
  • Click Deploy, copy the generated Web App URL, and paste it into the field on the left.
